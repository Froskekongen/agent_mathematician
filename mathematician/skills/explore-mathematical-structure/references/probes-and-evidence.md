# Advanced Probe Protocol

Read this reference for material computation, solver work, formal checking, or
parallel scouts. Keep raw probe and scout records in the round's temporary
workpad. Before closing, route their useful conclusions according to the
[research-memory protocol](../../research-mathematics/references/research-memory.md).

## Discriminate efficiently

Choose the smallest case likely to separate the live predictions: a boundary
or degeneracy, controlled deformation, exhaustive small family, adversarial or
held-out case, equivalent presentation, null baseline, or a population scan
paired with one microscope witness. Match the executor to the uncertainty.

For each material probe, preserve in the workpad:

- the pinned candidate definitions, affected literal claims, competing prior
  predictions, and nearest alternative explanation;
- the exact objects, parameter range, generation or sampling procedure,
  randomness, precision, and stopping rule;
- the executor, implementation, dependency versions, and raw outcome;
- the mathematical proposition actually supported or opposed, its coverage,
  and its correspondence to the intended objects; and
- artifact risks, known-case checks, independent cross-checks, and the
  resulting research decision.

Test known cases, distinguish implementation failure from mathematical
failure, and independently cross-check a decisive result when feasible. A
computation establishes only the encoded problem on its recorded coverage; a
formal checker establishes only the encoded statement. A numerical failure is
not a counterexample until mathematical and implementation checks validate the
witness.

Promote a decisive result and its load-bearing scope into the canonical
document. Preserve a certificate, program, or dataset as a native artifact
when later inspection is necessary. Create a research-memory card only for a
reusable noncanonical result—for example, an expensive negative screen, a
structural obstruction, or a residual probe with a concrete next test. Its
summary must state the mathematical content and scope without requiring the
raw workpad. Discard routine probe mechanics after successful consolidation.

## Bound scouts

The first tracer normally identifies scout lanes without dispatching them.
Dispatch independent candidate, literature, falsifier, or computation lanes
only when the user asks, a one-shot result requires them, or an explicit budget
covers them. Freeze the formalism, target, and report shape before dispatch.
Each scout returns a content-bound report to the coordinator; scouts never
write the canonical document or any research-memory database. Agent agreement
is not evidence.

On surprise, verify artifacts and inspect the smallest witness. On conflict,
retain both results in the workpad and seek an independent probe. After two
review cycles without new evidence, mark the conflict unresolved and state its
effect on the canonical recommendation. Card it only when the conflict is
costly or likely to recur, with a concrete resolution test. On stall, change
the representation, example family, scale, or executor, or return a semantic
wedge question.
