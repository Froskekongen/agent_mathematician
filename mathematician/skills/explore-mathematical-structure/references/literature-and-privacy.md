# Literature and Privacy Protocol

Read this reference when any source enters the round, terminology, prior art,
or novelty affects the decision, or before major investment in an apparently
new direction. Use the canonical `Q#`, `S#`, and `E#` records in
[the ledger protocol](exploration-ledger.md); a user-supplied source needs
`S#` and `E#`, not a fictitious `Q#`.

For sensitive unpublished work, record a mode before source exposure:
`EXACT` searches the construction, `ABSTRACTED` searches only an approved
abstraction, and `NONE` performs no external search. Ask only when privacy
expectations or safe abstraction are unclear and consequential.

Use a bounded cold pass when independent candidates matter, preserving their
pre-source versions. Use a warm pass for established terminology, equivalent
constructions, exact theorems and hypotheses, counterexamples, classifications,
or prior formulations. Record queries or abstracted scope, source classes,
date, stopping scope, exact citations, and imported statements with hypotheses.

Classify searched-scope novelty as: known under the same formulation; known
under another representation; transfer or synthesis; apparently distinct; or
unresolved because coverage is incomplete. State “no prior art found in the
searched sources,” never an unqualified novelty claim.

A located source supports only what it literally states. Promote
`SOURCE-LOCATED` to `SOURCE-APPLICABLE` only after explicitly mapping its
objects and assumptions to pinned local versions and recording unmatched
hypotheses.
