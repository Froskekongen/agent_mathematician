# Literature and Privacy Protocol

Read this reference when any source enters the round, terminology, prior art,
or novelty affects the decision, or before major investment in an apparently
new direction. Keep queries, coverage notes, source triage, and cold-pass
drafts in the temporary workpad. Route durable results according to the
[research-memory protocol](../../research-mathematics/references/research-memory.md).

## Protect unpublished work

For sensitive unpublished work, choose a mode before source exposure:

- `EXACT`: search the construction as stated;
- `ABSTRACTED`: search only an approved abstraction; or
- `NONE`: perform no external search.

Ask only when privacy expectations or a safe abstraction are unclear and
consequential. Record the chosen mode and the exact or abstracted search scope
in the workpad. Never place private query text or unpublished details in a
research-memory card merely to preserve search history.

## Search and test applicability

Use a bounded cold pass when independent candidates matter, preserving their
pre-source versions in the workpad. Use a warm pass for established
terminology, equivalent constructions, exact theorems and hypotheses,
counterexamples, classifications, or prior formulations.

Record the date, query or approved abstraction, source classes, stopping
scope, exact citations, and imported statements with their hypotheses. A
user-supplied source needs an applicability analysis but no invented search
history.

Classify searched-scope novelty as one of:

- known under the same formulation;
- known under another representation;
- transfer or synthesis;
- apparently distinct; or
- unresolved because coverage is incomplete.

State “no prior art found in the searched sources,” never an unqualified
novelty claim.

A located source supports only what it literally states. Treat it as
applicable only after explicitly mapping its objects, maps, assumptions, and
scope to the current local definitions and recording every unmatched
hypothesis. Preserve exact citations in the canonical document when they
support accepted or load-bearing mathematics.

Create a `source-applicability` research-memory card when a noncanonical
mapping, mismatch, or negative result will materially inform a later route.
The card must include the imported statement, local mapping, unmatched
hypotheses, applicability conclusion, source locator, and enough scope to be
understood independently. Do not preserve routine query logs or lists of
irrelevant results after successful consolidation.
