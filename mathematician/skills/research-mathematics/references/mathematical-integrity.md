# Shared Mathematical Integrity

Read this reference for every mathematical skill. It protects meaning and
epistemic calibration while leaving room for fast intuition, analogy, examples,
computation, conjecture, and partial arguments. It is not a proof protocol: the
active skill supplies its task-local completion gate, and only
`research-mathematics` applies the full claim-resolution chain.

A contribution is **load-bearing** when changing or removing it could change an
exact claim, recommendation, status, or the reader's mathematical model.

## Fidelity

Keep every load-bearing mathematical referent identifiable. State objects,
maps, domains, quantifiers, conventions, and assumptions to the precision
needed for their present use. Separate the literal statement, intended meaning,
and any repaired or restricted version. Name candidate versions when several
remain live; a material change creates a new candidate rather than silently
changing the old one.

Treat prose, formulas, diagrams, source statements, and executable or formal
encodings as distinct representations. When a conclusion crosses between them,
make the correspondence and any unmatched structure explicit.

## Warrant

Make the role and evidential force of each load-bearing contribution visible
where a reader could mistake it: definition, imported result, deduction,
checked example, counterexample, computation, heuristic, analogy, conjecture,
or proof sketch. Record the relevant scope and trust boundary.

Match the conclusion to the warrant:

- an example establishes its instance and may expose a mechanism;
- a checked counterexample can refute the exact claim it negates;
- computation supports only the faithfully encoded proposition on its checked
  scope;
- analogy and heuristic reasoning can motivate, organize, or predict, but do
  not establish an unresolved claim; and
- failure to find a defect establishes only survival within the reported
  search.

Check any displayed calculation, example, source application, or translation
that materially carries the response. An imported theorem supports only its
exact statement after its hypotheses and conventions match the present use.

## Recoverable intuition

Use mental models, pictures, examples, and metaphors freely when they reveal
structure. Keep them mathematically recoverable: identify the exact objects or
relations they organize, the correspondence being used, and the structure it
preserves. For a load-bearing analogy, also name the relevant mismatch or
breakpoint. A checked anchor and boundary case are often the cheapest recovery
tests.

For an unresolved claim that materially depends on a heuristic step, state a
**conversion obligation**: the smallest exact definition, lemma, source match,
scope extension, or check needed before that step can warrant the stronger exact
conclusion. The obligation records the bridge back to rigorous mathematics; it
is not evidence that the bridge has been crossed.

## Calibration

Use conclusive language only at the strength licensed by the warrant and the
active skill's local gate. Keep mathematical truth status separate from
plausibility, confidence, workflow disposition, explanatory value, and source
provenance. Explanation, rewriting, translation, and consolidation inherit
rather than upgrade source status.

Spend rigor in proportion to what the response asks the reader to believe or
act on. Close routine local claims with local checks. When the available warrant
does not reach the requested conclusion, return the strongest honest qualified
result and the exact open or conversion obligations instead.
